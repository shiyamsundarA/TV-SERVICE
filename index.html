About Us

Who We Are

Berts Electronics is a trusted TV repair and sales service provider in Thoothukudi, specializing in LED, LCD, Smart, Android, and UHD TVs. With years of industry experience, our skilled technicians deliver fast, reliable, and affordable repair solutions for all major brands.

Why Choose Us

From screen and sound issues to complete TV servicing, we provide high-quality on-site and in-store support. We are committed to honest pricing, reliable repairs, and excellent customer service.

What we do

Service

From flickering panels to complete board replacement — we fix it all, fast and honestly priced.

LED / LCD Panel Repair

Screen lines, dead pixels, backlight failure, no display — diagnosed in-house with genuine spare parts and a 3-month service warranty.

  • Backlight replacement
  • T-Con & main board repair
  • Panel diagnostics

TV Wall Mounting

Expert TV wall mounting services for all TV brands with precision installation, clean cable routing, and a hassle-free setup.

  • Compatible with all TV sizes & brands
  • Secure bracket installation
  • Cable concealment & final setup demo

Branded Home Theater Service

Professional home theater solutions for premium brands, ensuring crystal-clear audio, seamless connectivity, and long-lasting performance.

  • Installation & configuration
  • Woofer, amplifier & speaker repair
  • Complete system inspection & testing

Doorstep Pickup & Delivery

Can't move the TV? Our delivery team picks it up, brings it to the workshop, and returns it fully tested — free within city limits.

  • Safe packaging
  • Insured transit
  • Free within 10 km
